For Millennia, the Sunicorns and the Rainicorns have been in conflict.  Each tries to destroy the other, but without both Sun and Rain there is no Rainbow.  You have to keep both sides in balance as long as possible.

Use your magic powers to slow the winner down and help the under-corn.  You can Sparklify, Freeze, Stealth, Berserk, or Turncoat unicorns.  But you have to move fast, because both sides are getting better and faster the longer you play, they will learn to use the same powers, and it's easy to tilt the balance too far.  

Click to use your powers, with keyboard shortcuts to change powers quickly.
